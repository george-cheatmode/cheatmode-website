FAQ

Something isn't working. What should I do?

Update the app to the latest version and restart your device. If the problem continues, email support@cheatmode.llc with the details above. Screenshots help.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases made through the App Store or Google Play are refunded by the store. Request a refund from Apple or Google directly.

How do I delete my data?

Delete your account in the app where offered, or email privacy@cheatmode.llc and tell us which app and what data. Details: deleting your data.

How do I change ad or tracking settings?

In the app, open Settings > Privacy settings. More options: your privacy choices.
