Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains how Cheat Mode LLC ("we", "us") handles personal data when you use our website, apps, games and services (together, the "Services"). Some Services may show additional privacy information, for example on their store listing or inside the app. Where they do, that information adds to this policy.

Jump to: privacy choices · delete data · GDPR rights · US state rights · children · contact

1. Who we are

Cheat Mode LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, is the controller of personal data processed through the Services. Company details are on our legal notice.

Privacy questions and requests: privacy@cheatmode.llc

2. Data we collect

Data you give us

For example when you contact us, request support, work with us, create an account, make a purchase or take part in a feature: your name, email address, the content of your messages, account details and any other information you choose to provide.

Data collected automatically

When you use the Services we and our partners may collect device and app information (such as device model, operating system, app version, language), identifiers (such as app instance IDs, advertising IDs and IDs set by SDKs), approximate location derived from your IP address, usage and diagnostic data (such as features used, crashes and performance), and ad interactions.

Data from others

We may receive data from app stores, platform providers, advertising and analytics partners, for example purchase confirmations, attribution data or aggregated statistics.

Do you have to give us data?

No law requires you to give us personal data. Some data is needed for a Service to work (for example an email address to answer you, or account details to run an account). Without it, that feature may not be available.

3. How we use data

  • To provide, maintain and improve the Services.
  • To answer your messages and provide support.
  • To deliver our consulting and development services to clients.
  • To understand how the Services are used, measure performance and fix problems (analytics).
  • To show ads, including personalised ads where you allow it, and to measure them.
  • To measure and improve our marketing, including attribution of installs.
  • To process purchases, prevent fraud and abuse, and keep the Services secure.
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR): providing a Service or support you asked for.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and national rules on device storage such as the ePrivacy Directive and Germany's TDDDG): personalised advertising, and analytics or tracking technologies that store or read information on your device where consent is required. You can withdraw consent at any time (see privacy choices). Withdrawal does not affect processing before it.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR): running, securing and improving the Services, fraud prevention, non-personalised ads, and answering business enquiries.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR): for example keeping tax records.

5. Sharing

  • Service providers (processors) that work on our behalf, such as hosting, analytics, crash reporting, email and payment providers. They may use the data only on our instructions.
  • Advertising and measurement partners, which may use identifiers and usage data to show and measure ads, including personalised ads where you allow it. Some act as independent controllers and may combine data with data from other apps and websites under their own policies.
  • App stores and platform providers, when you download or buy through them.
  • Authorities or other parties where required by law, or to protect our rights and users.
  • A successor, if our business or a Service is sold or reorganised.

We require every third party that receives personal data from us to protect it at a level at least equal to this policy and to the law that applies to it.

We do not sell personal data for money. Sharing data with advertising partners for personalised ads may count as "selling" or "sharing" under some US state laws. You can opt out (see privacy choices).

6. Profiling and automated decisions

Personalised advertising involves profiling: our ad partners may infer your interests from your device's activity to choose which ads to show. This happens only with your consent where the law requires it. We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

7. Cookies, SDKs and tracking

Our Services may use cookies, software development kits (SDKs) and similar technologies for analytics, advertising and the purposes above. Where required, we ask for your consent first. On iOS we track you across other companies' apps and websites only if you allow it through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt.

This website sets no cookies and uses no trackers or analytics.

Do Not Track: there is no common standard for browser "Do Not Track" signals, so our Services do not respond to them. We do honour Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out of sale and sharing (see below).

8. Your privacy choices

  • In our apps: open Settings > Privacy settings to review or change your ad and analytics consent at any time.
  • Do not sell or share: to opt out of personalised ads and the "sale" or "sharing" of your data, turn off personalised ads in the app's Privacy settings, or email privacy@cheatmode.llc with the subject "Do not sell or share". We also treat a Global Privacy Control signal as an opt-out where it reaches us.
  • iPhone and iPad: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, to allow or refuse tracking per app.
  • Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Google > Ads), to reset or delete your advertising ID.
  • Marketing emails: we send them only with your consent, and every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Notifications: turn them off at any time in your device settings.

9. Deleting your data

If a Service has accounts, you can delete your account inside the app where offered, or by emailing privacy@cheatmode.llc from the address linked to the account. We delete the account and its personal data within 30 days, except data we must keep by law (for example purchase records). For any other data, email privacy@cheatmode.llc and tell us which Service and what you want deleted. Deleting an app from your device removes the data stored on that device.

10. Your rights (EEA, UK, Switzerland and similar laws)

Under the GDPR, UK GDPR, the Swiss FADP and similar laws you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and get a copy (Art. 15);
  • have it corrected (Art. 16) or deleted (Art. 17);
  • restrict its processing (Art. 18);
  • receive it in a portable format (Art. 20);
  • object at any time to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing and related profiling (Art. 21);
  • withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3));
  • complain to a data protection authority, for example in the country where you live or work (Art. 77).

To exercise a right, email privacy@cheatmode.llc. We answer within one month, which may be extended by two months for complex requests. We may need to verify your identity. Using your rights is free.

11. US state privacy rights

This section applies to residents of California and other US states with consumer privacy laws (such as Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Oregon, Texas, Utah and Virginia). It is also our notice at collection under California law.

  • Categories collected in the past 12 months: identifiers (device and advertising IDs, email address); internet or app activity; approximate geolocation; commercial information (purchases); information you send us. Sources: you, your device, and our partners. Purposes: section 3. Recipients: section 5. Retention: section 14.
  • Sale and sharing: advertising partners may receive identifiers and app activity, which may count as "selling" or "sharing" for targeted advertising. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of consumers under 16.
  • Sensitive personal information: we do not collect it to infer characteristics about you, so the right to limit its use does not apply.
  • Your rights: to know, access, correct and delete your personal information; to get a portable copy; to opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising and profiling; and not to be discriminated against for using these rights.
  • Financial incentives: we do not offer any in exchange for personal information.
  • Direct marketing disclosure (California "Shine the Light"): we do not share personal information with third parties for their own direct marketing.

To make a request, email privacy@cheatmode.llc. An authorised agent may make a request for you with your written permission. We respond within 45 days. If we deny a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with the subject "Appeal". If the appeal is denied, you may contact your state attorney general.

12. Children

Our Services are not directed at children under 13 in the United States, or under the age of digital consent in your country (13 to 16 in the EU and UK), unless a Service says otherwise. We do not knowingly collect personal data from such children. If a Service is ever directed at children, we will follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar laws, including obtaining verifiable parental consent where required and not showing personalised ads to children.

Parents or guardians who believe their child has given us personal data can email privacy@cheatmode.llc, and we will delete it.

13. Other countries

If you live in a country with its own privacy law (for example Canada, Brazil, Australia, Japan, South Korea or India), you have the rights that law gives you, and you can use them by emailing privacy@cheatmode.llc.

14. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymise it:

  • support and business emails: up to 3 years after our last contact;
  • account data: until you delete the account, then 30 days;
  • analytics data: up to 14 months;
  • purchase and tax records: as long as tax law requires (usually up to 7 years).

15. International transfers

We are based in the United States. Your data may be processed in the United States and other countries. For transfers out of the EEA, UK or Switzerland we rely on adequacy decisions (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where our provider is certified) or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for a copy of the safeguards at privacy@cheatmode.llc.

16. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities where the law requires.

17. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for material changes, give notice through the Services where appropriate.
